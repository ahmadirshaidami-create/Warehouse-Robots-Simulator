What is this simulation?

This is a fulfilment centre: it stores products, and when a customer orders online it picks, packs and hands the parcel to the delivery company.

⚠️ The problemOn busy days pickers walk ~30 km, make mistakes, and orders miss the courier.
🤖 The technologyGoods-to-person robots bring whole shelves to a worker, and an AI decides who does what, in which order and when to charge.
❓ The questionShould our Amman fulfilment centre invest in robots, and how many?

You will choose a day (normal, White Friday, power cut…), watch the warehouse live with and without robots, then get the result and a recommendation.
